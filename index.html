Research experience: undergraduate thesis

Development of an AR-based online shopping system for wearable products

B.Sc. thesis, Department of CSE, CUET, July 2023. Supervised by Md. Sabir Hossain, Assistant Professor.

Shoppers cannot try on fashion items online, which drives high return rates. I built an Android shopping app where users try products on live through the front camera, and where a recommender suggests other items to try next. The system covers eyeglasses, caps and hats, and jewellery, so that one interface handles several product categories.

Augmented try-on

The try-on screen uses Unity3D AR Foundation with the ARCore Augmented Faces API. The 468-point face mesh and the center and region poses anchor each 3D model to the face, so glasses sit on the nose bridge, hats on the head, and earrings and nose rings at their landmarks.

Hybrid recommendation

Recommendations are a weighted sum of two methods. Content-based filtering applies TF-IDF and cosine similarity to product attributes. User-based collaborative filtering uses mean-centered purchase counts, Pearson correlation, a similarity threshold of 0.3, and the ten nearest neighbors.

System design

A client-server architecture keeps the AR rendering on the device and the recommender on a backend service, connected through an API gateway.

Limitations and future work

The transaction history is a custom dataset and the evaluation is offline, so a study with real users is the natural next step. I would also extend try-on to clothing, shoes, and watches, use body measurements for size-aware recommendations, and generate 3D models from 2D photos to reduce manual asset preparation.
